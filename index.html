Lets Test Together

An Android app by Pradeep V Chelani

Lets Test Together is a free feedback exchange for Android developers. Try other developers' apps, share what you honestly think, and earn points that you spend on getting your own app tested.

How it works

  • Sign in with Google and receive 50 welcome points.
  • Pick an app on Discover, install it from Google Play and use it.
  • Give a star rating and written feedback, then complete the test to earn 5 points.
  • List your own app and fund its own points wallet. Each finished test is paid from that wallet.

Good to know

  • Points are only for use inside the app. They have no cash value and cannot be withdrawn.
  • The app is a community for exchanging feedback. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Google.
