Delete your account

Lets Test Together – Pradeep V Chelani · Last updated: October 21, 2026

Delete it yourself in the app

  1. Open Lets Test Together and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Scroll to Danger zone and tap Delete my account.
  4. Confirm. Your account is deleted immediately.

Can't open the app?

Email pradeepchelani@gmail.com from the Google account you used to sign in, with the subject Delete my Lets Test Together account. We will delete your account and reply to confirm, within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your profile, name and email address.
  • Your points balance and point history.
  • The apps you listed, including their wallets and icons.
  • The tests you took and the feedback you wrote, and the feedback other testers left on your apps.

What is kept

Nothing that identifies you is kept after deletion. Routine backups made by our database provider may hold copies for a short time before they are removed in the normal cycle.

Points are not real money, so deleting your account does not refund them. This cannot be undone.
