Privacy Policy

Lets Test Together – Pradeep V Chelani · Last updated: October 21, 2026

Application Covered

This policy applies to the Lets Test Together Android app (package name com.pradeep.letstesttogether), published by Pradeep V Chelani.

Information We Collect

When you sign in with Google, we receive and store:

  • Your Google account name and email address.
  • A unique account identifier created for your account.

While you use the app, we also store what you create inside it:

  • Your points balance and a short history of point changes (kept for 7 days).
  • Apps you list: the app name, package name, Google Play links, description, icon and the points in its wallet.
  • Tests you take: which app, when you started and finished, the star rating and the written feedback you submit.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, files, advertising ID, device identifiers or any information about which other apps you use. The app does not contain ads or analytics trackers.

How We Use Information

  • To sign you in and keep your account and points correct.
  • To show listed apps to testers and to show feedback to the developer of the app that was tested.
  • To prevent misuse, such as completing a test twice or spending points you do not have.

What Other Users Can See

When you complete a test, the developer of that app can see your first name, your star rating and your written feedback. When you list an app, testers can see the app's name, description, icon and your display name. Your email address is not shown to other users.

Data Storage

Your data is stored in a managed database provided by Supabase. Access is restricted so that each user can only read and change what belongs to them, apart from the information described above that is shared on purpose. Data is sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

Third-Party Services

  • Google Sign-In to let you sign in with your Google account.
  • Google Play, which opens when you follow links to install or test an app.
  • Supabase for sign-in, database and image storage.

These services have their own privacy policies.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it with advertisers. It is shared only with the service providers listed above, as needed to run the app, or if the law requires it.

Retention and Deletion

We keep your data while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time inside the app (Profile, then Delete my account), or by following the steps on the account deletion page. Deleting your account permanently removes your profile, points, apps, tests and feedback.

Children's Privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

We use reasonable technical measures to protect your data, including encrypted connections and database rules that limit who can read and change each record. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed, and the current version is always available at this page.
